# Collection-2

Build a single-file HTML first-person walkable hotel corridor in three.js.

LAYOUT
- Straight corridor, 2.4 m wide, 2.7 m ceiling, about 45 m long.
- Both ends fade into dark fog at the very far distance with no visible end wall, so it feels endless.
- Doorways on alternating sides, about 6 m apart.
There are doors with the numbers: 22, 1930, 1944, 127, 1031, 9, 10, and the others door numbers are random. The numbers are on small brass plates at eye level.
- Each doorway is recessed 0.15 m into the wall with a dark wood frame so it has visible thickness. 

SURFACES
- Floor: carpet texture I will supply. 
  Make it tileable, with repeat scale as a parameter, and add slight
  darkening along the wall edges.
- Walls: wallpaper fileable pattern I will provide. Baseboard and simple crown molding.
- Ceiling: flat, with flush rectangular light fixtures every 4 m,
  color to match the background of the wallpaper, with pools of light and darker gaps between.
- No people, signs, or props except the door number plates.

CAMERA AND LOOK
- First person at 1.6 m, WASD plus mouse look
