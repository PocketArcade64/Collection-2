Populations are refreshed monthly by PriceCharting, and half grades fold into the nearest whole grade, so an 8.5 counts in 8.

The PSA 10 column is PSA-specific, but the Grade 9 column is a blended price covering PSA, BGS and CGC slabs. A true PSA 9 usually sells above a CGC 9, so treat the Grade 9 figures as slightly soft. Prices in italics are PriceCharting estimates rather than recorded sales, which is common at PSA 10 on cards nobody has sold recently.

Where a card has no PSA figure, the screener estimates it at 45% of the combined PSA and CGC count. That is deliberately generous: the one card measured directly, Gardevoir, came in at 28%. A card is only flagged as worth a population check if even that generous estimate lands under your limit and the price already clears.
