Exporting

Your video is a WebM file

To convert it to MP4 with no visible loss, run this in a terminal with ffmpeg installed (or open it in HandBrake and choose H.264):

ffmpeg -i hoots_wide_eyed_wonders.webm -c:v libx264 -crf 14 -preset slow -pix_fmt yuv420p -r 24 -c:a aac -b:a 256k hoots_wide_eyed_wonders.mp4

For the exact soundtrack, mux the WAV instead: add -i hoots_wide_eyed_wonders.wav -map 0:v -map 1:a before the output name.
